Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

"Tonys Agent Stats" is used only by the owner of the Tony's Agent accounts, for their own accounts.

  • What it reads: basic profile info and public statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares, follower count).
  • Where it is kept: as numbers in the owner's private project, used to decide which video ideas work best.
  • Sharing: no data is sold or shared with anyone. No data from other users is collected.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings, and the stored numbers can be deleted by the owner.

Questions: message Tony's Agent on Instagram or TikTok.
