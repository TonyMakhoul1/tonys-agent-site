Why AI says yes to everything

AI is trained to please you, so it often calls a bad idea brilliant. That's called sycophancy.

  1. Don't ask "Is my idea good?". It will almost always say yes.
  2. Ask: "Give me the 3 biggest reasons this could fail."
  3. Ask it to play a tough critic, investor or customer who doesn't like the idea.
  4. Don't say which answer you're hoping for. It will lean toward it.
  5. Push back once ("Are you sure?"). If it flips right away, its first answer was just being nice.

Try this today: Paste your next idea and ask: "Be brutally honest. What's the weakest part, and what would make it fail?"
