Stop getting boring AI answers

A vague question gets a vague answer. Add three things and the answer gets 10× better.

  1. WHO it's for: "a gift for my dad, 60, who loves fishing and old cars".
  2. WHAT they like or what you want: style, tone, examples you love.
  3. The LIMIT: budget, length, deadline. "Under $50, 3 ideas, one sentence each."
  4. Ask for options: "Give me 5 different ideas" beats "give me an idea".
  5. Don't like it? Say why ("too formal", "too expensive") and ask again.

Try this today: Rewrite your last AI question with who + likes + limit, and compare the two answers.
