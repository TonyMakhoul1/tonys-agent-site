Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

"Tonys Agent Stats" (also shown as Tony's Agent Studio) is used only by the owner of the Tony's Agent accounts, for their own accounts.

  • What it reads: basic profile info (nickname, username, avatar), the account's posting options, and public statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares, follower count).
  • What it posts: only the owner's own finished Tony's Agent episodes, to the owner's own TikTok account, after the owner reviews each video, writes or confirms the caption, and chooses who can view it and whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed.
  • Where it is kept: the access key and the owner's posting settings stay on the owner's own computer; statistics are kept as numbers in the owner's private project, used to decide which video ideas work best.
  • Sharing: no data is sold or shared with anyone. No data from other users is collected.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings (Security and permissions, Apps and services), and the stored key, settings and numbers can be deleted by the owner.

Questions: message Tony's Agent on Instagram or TikTok.
